Checking the MCQ Form Builder connector...

To make forms from here, add MCQ Form Builder as a connector in Claude. You only do this once.

Connector URL https://mcq-formbuilder.vercel.app/mcp
  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it MCQ Form Builder exactly and paste the URL above.
  3. Click Connect, sign in with your Google account and choose Allow.
  4. Come back here and press Make the Google Form.
