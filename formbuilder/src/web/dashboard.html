Connect Claude.Make forms straight from the builder.

Add this app as a connector in Claude once. The MCQ Form Builder artifact then makes the Google Form for you with one click, and every form shows up above.

Connector URL Paste this when Claude asks for the server URL.
  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it MCQ Form Builder exactly, so the builder can find it, and paste the URL above.
  3. Click Connect, sign in with this Google account and choose Allow.
  4. Open the MCQ Form Builder and press Make the Google Form.
